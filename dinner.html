Creamy Tuscan Chicken

Ingredients:

2 chicken breasts; 150 ml cooking cream; 100 g sun-dried tomatoes; 2 garlic cloves; 50 g Parmesan; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp Italian herbs; 1 handful fresh spinach; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Season the chicken breasts with salt, pepper, and Italian herbs.

2. Heat olive oil in a large pan and cook the chicken for 5–6 minutes on each side.

3. Remove the chicken and sauté the garlic and sun-dried tomatoes for 2 minutes.

4. Add the cream and Parmesan and stir until the sauce becomes creamy.

5. Add the spinach and cook for 1–2 minutes.

6. Return the chicken to the pan and simmer for another 3–4 minutes.

Creamy Mushroom Pasta

Ingredients:

250 g pasta; 250 g mushrooms, sliced; 150 ml cooking cream; 2 garlic cloves; 40 g Parmesan; 1 tbsp butter; 1 tsp dried thyme; salt and pepper; fresh parsley.

How to make it:

1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions and reserve ½ cup of pasta water.

2. Melt the butter in a large pan.

3. Add the mushrooms and cook for 5–7 minutes until golden.

4. Add the garlic and thyme and cook for another minute.

5. Pour in the cream and Parmesan and stir until smooth.

6. Add the pasta and a little pasta water, then toss everything together and garnish with parsley.

Honey Garlic Salmon

Ingredients:

2 salmon fillets; 2 tbsp honey; 1 tbsp soy sauce; 2 garlic cloves, minced; 1 tsp lemon juice; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper; fresh parsley.

How to make it:

1. Mix honey, soy sauce, garlic, and lemon juice in a small bowl.

2. Season the salmon with salt and pepper.

3. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.

4. Cook the salmon skin-side down for 4–5 minutes.

5. Flip the salmon and pour the honey garlic sauce over it.

6. Cook for another 3–4 minutes, spooning the sauce over the salmon. Garnish with parsley.

Cheesy Chicken Bake

Ingredients:

2 chicken breasts; 150 g mozzarella; 50 g Parmesan; 200 g cherry tomatoes; 1 zucchini; 2 garlic cloves; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp Italian herbs; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F).

2. Cut the chicken and zucchini into bite-sized pieces.

3. Place chicken, zucchini, tomatoes, and garlic in a baking dish.

4. Add olive oil, Italian herbs, salt, and pepper and mix well.

5. Bake for 20–25 minutes.

6. Add mozzarella and Parmesan on top and bake for another 8–10 minutes until golden and melted.

Creamy Garlic Shrimp

Ingredients:

300 g peeled shrimp; 150 ml cooking cream; 3 garlic cloves; 30 g Parmesan; 1 tbsp butter; 1 tbsp lemon juice; 1 tsp paprika; salt and pepper; fresh parsley.

How to make it:

1. Season the shrimp with paprika, salt, and pepper.

2. Melt the butter in a pan and cook the shrimp for 2–3 minutes on each side.

3. Remove the shrimp and sauté the garlic for 1 minute.

4. Add the cream and Parmesan and stir until creamy.

5. Return the shrimp to the pan and add lemon juice.

6. Simmer for 2 minutes and garnish with fresh parsley.

Vegetable Coconut Curry

Ingredients:

1 zucchini; 1 bell pepper; 1 carrot; 1 cup cauliflower; 1 can coconut milk; 1 can diced tomatoes; 1 onion; 2 garlic cloves; 1 tbsp curry powder; 1 tsp cumin; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Heat olive oil in a large pan and sauté the onion for 4–5 minutes.

2. Add garlic, curry powder, and cumin and cook for 1 minute.

3. Add the zucchini, pepper, carrot, and cauliflower.

4. Cook the vegetables for 5 minutes.

5. Add the tomatoes and coconut milk and simmer for 15–20 minutes.

6. Season with salt and pepper and serve with rice.

Beef Teriyaki Rice Bowl

Ingredients:

250 g beef strips; 150 g cooked rice; 1 carrot; ½ bell pepper; 2 spring onions; 3 tbsp teriyaki sauce; 1 tsp sesame oil; 1 tbsp olive oil; sesame seeds.

How to make it:

1. Slice the carrot and bell pepper into thin strips.

2. Heat olive oil in a large pan.

3. Add the beef and cook for 3–4 minutes until browned.

4. Add the vegetables and stir-fry for another 3–4 minutes.

5. Pour in the teriyaki sauce and sesame oil and cook for 1–2 minutes.

6. Serve over warm rice and garnish with spring onions and sesame seeds.

Baked Pesto Gnocchi

Ingredients:

400 g gnocchi; 150 g cherry tomatoes; 3 tbsp pesto; 150 g mozzarella; 30 g Parmesan; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper; fresh basil.

How to make it:

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F).

2. Place the gnocchi and cherry tomatoes in a baking dish.

3. Add pesto, olive oil, salt, and pepper and mix well.

4. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan.

5. Bake for 20–25 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbling.

6. Garnish with fresh basil and serve hot.
