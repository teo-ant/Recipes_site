Fluffy Banana Pancakes

Ingredients:

1 ripe banana; 1 egg; 100 g flour; 100 ml milk; 1 tsp baking powder; 1 tsp vanilla extract; 1 tbsp sugar; a pinch of salt.

How to make it:

1. Mash the banana in a bowl.

2. Add the egg, milk, vanilla, and sugar and mix well.

3. Stir in the flour, baking powder, and salt until just combined.

4. Heat a lightly buttered pan over medium heat.

5. Pour small portions of batter into the pan and cook for 2–3 minutes on each side.

6. Serve warm with fruit, honey, or maple syrup.

Avocado & Egg Toast

Ingredients:

2 slices of bread; 1 ripe avocado; 2 eggs; 1 tsp lemon juice; ½ tsp chili flakes; salt and pepper; 1 tsp olive oil.

How to make it:

1. Toast the bread until golden and crispy.

2. Mash the avocado with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.

3. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the eggs to your preference.

4. Spread the mashed avocado over the toast.

5. Place one egg on each slice.

6. Sprinkle with chili flakes and serve immediately.

Berry Overnight Oats

Ingredients:

80 g rolled oats; 150 ml milk; 100 g Greek yogurt; 1 tbsp honey; ½ tsp vanilla extract; 80 g mixed berries; 1 tbsp chia seeds.

How to make it:

1. Add oats, milk, Greek yogurt, honey, vanilla, and chia seeds to a jar.

2. Stir everything together until well combined.

3. Add half of the berries and mix gently.

4. Cover the jar and refrigerate overnight.

5. In the morning, stir the oats and add a little more milk if needed.

6. Top with the remaining berries and serve cold.

Cheesy Breakfast Quesadilla

Ingredients:

2 flour tortillas; 2 eggs; 80 g grated cheddar; ½ bell pepper, diced; 2 tbsp sweet corn; 1 tbsp butter; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Whisk the eggs with salt and pepper.

2. Melt the butter in a pan and scramble the eggs.

3. Place one tortilla in a clean pan over medium heat.

4. Add half of the cheese, scrambled eggs, bell pepper, corn, and the remaining cheese.

5. Cover with the second tortilla and cook for 2–3 minutes on each side.

6. Cut into triangles and serve warm.

French Toast with Berries

Ingredients:

4 slices of bread; 2 eggs; 100 ml milk; 1 tsp vanilla extract; ½ tsp cinnamon; 1 tbsp butter; 100 g mixed berries; 1 tbsp maple syrup.

How to make it:

1. Whisk the eggs, milk, vanilla, and cinnamon in a shallow bowl.

2. Dip each slice of bread into the mixture for a few seconds on both sides.

3. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat.

4. Cook the bread for 2–3 minutes on each side until golden.

5. Place the French toast on a plate.

6. Top with berries and drizzle with maple syrup.

Creamy Berry Smoothie Bowl

Ingredients:

1 frozen banana; 150 g frozen mixed berries; 100 g Greek yogurt; 50 ml milk; 1 tsp honey; 2 tbsp granola; fresh fruit for topping.

How to make it:

1. Add the frozen banana, berries, Greek yogurt, milk, and honey to a blender.

2. Blend until thick and creamy.

3. Add a little more milk if the mixture is too thick to blend.

4. Pour the smoothie into a bowl.

5. Top with granola and fresh fruit.

6. Serve immediately.

Ham & Cheese Breakfast Muffins

Ingredients:

3 eggs; 100 g flour; 1 tsp baking powder; 100 ml milk; 80 g grated cheese; 80 g diced ham; ½ bell pepper, diced; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and grease a muffin tin.

2. Whisk the eggs and milk together.

3. Add flour, baking powder, salt, and pepper and mix until combined.

4. Fold in the cheese, ham, and bell pepper.

5. Divide the mixture evenly between the muffin cups.

6. Bake for 18–20 minutes until golden and cooked through.

Apple Cinnamon Breakfast Oatmeal

Ingredients:

80 g rolled oats; 250 ml milk; 1 apple, diced; 1 tsp cinnamon; 1 tbsp honey; 1 tbsp chopped walnuts; a pinch of salt.

How to make it:

1. Add oats, milk, and salt to a saucepan.

2. Cook over medium heat for 4–5 minutes, stirring occasionally.

3. Add the diced apple and cinnamon.

4. Cook for another 2–3 minutes until the apple softens.

5. Transfer the oatmeal to a bowl and drizzle with honey.

6. Top with chopped walnuts and serve warm.
