Chickpea Curry

Ingredients:

2 cans chickpeas, drained; 1 can coconut milk; 1 can diced tomatoes; 1 onion, chopped; 3 garlic cloves, minced; 1 tbsp curry powder; 1 tsp ground cumin; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper; fresh cilantro.

How to make it:

1. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.

2. Add the onion and cook for 4–5 minutes until soft.

3. Add garlic, curry powder, and cumin, then cook for 1 minute.

4. Stir in tomatoes, coconut milk, and chickpeas.

5. Simmer for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened.

6. Season with salt and pepper and garnish with fresh cilantro.

Tofu Stir-Fry

Ingredients:

400 g firm tofu, cubed; 1 bell pepper, sliced; 1 carrot, sliced; 1 broccoli head, chopped; 2 garlic cloves, minced; 2 tbsp soy sauce; 1 tbsp sesame oil; 1 tbsp maple syrup; 1 tbsp vegetable oil; sesame seeds.

How to make it:

1. Pat the tofu dry and cut it into bite-sized cubes.

2. Heat vegetable oil in a large frying pan and cook the tofu until golden.

3. Add broccoli, carrot, and bell pepper and stir-fry for 5–6 minutes.

4. Add garlic and cook for another minute.

5. Mix soy sauce, sesame oil, and maple syrup, then pour over the vegetables and tofu.

6. Stir well and serve topped with sesame seeds.

Lentil Soup

Ingredients:

1 cup dried green or brown lentils; 1 onion, chopped; 2 carrots, diced; 2 celery stalks, diced; 3 garlic cloves, minced; 1 can diced tomatoes; 5 cups vegetable broth; 1 tsp cumin; 1 tsp dried thyme; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot and sauté the onion, carrots, and celery for 5 minutes.

2. Add garlic, cumin, and thyme and cook for 1 minute.

3. Add lentils, tomatoes, and vegetable broth.

4. Bring the soup to a boil, then reduce the heat.

5. Simmer for 25–30 minutes until the lentils are tender.

6. Season with salt and pepper and serve hot.

Pasta Primavera

Ingredients:

300 g pasta; 1 zucchini, sliced; 1 bell pepper, sliced; 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved; 1 cup broccoli florets; 2 garlic cloves, minced; 2 tbsp olive oil; ½ tsp dried Italian herbs; salt and pepper; fresh basil.

How to make it:

1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions and reserve ½ cup of pasta water.

2. Heat olive oil in a large pan.

3. Add zucchini, bell pepper, and broccoli and cook for 5–7 minutes.

4. Add garlic and cherry tomatoes and cook for 2 minutes.

5. Add the cooked pasta, Italian herbs, and a little pasta water, then toss everything together.

6. Season with salt and pepper and garnish with fresh basil.

Black Bean Tacos

Ingredients:

8 small corn or flour tortillas; 2 cans black beans, drained; 1 avocado, sliced; 1 tomato, diced; ½ red onion, diced; 1 tsp cumin; ½ tsp chili powder; 1 lime; fresh cilantro; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Heat the black beans in a pan over medium heat.

2. Add cumin, chili powder, salt, and pepper and cook for 4–5 minutes.

3. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan for about 30 seconds on each side.

4. Fill each tortilla with the seasoned black beans.

5. Add avocado, tomato, and red onion.

6. Squeeze lime juice over the tacos and garnish with cilantro.

Buddha Bowl

Ingredients:

1 cup cooked quinoa; 1 can chickpeas, drained; 1 sweet potato, cubed; 1 cup broccoli; 1 avocado; 2 cups spinach; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp paprika; salt and pepper; 2 tbsp tahini; 1 tbsp lemon juice.

How to make it:

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F).

2. Toss the sweet potato and chickpeas with olive oil, paprika, salt, and pepper.

3. Roast them for 25–30 minutes, adding the broccoli during the final 10 minutes.

4. Prepare the tahini dressing by mixing tahini with lemon juice and a little water.

5. Divide quinoa and spinach between bowls and arrange the roasted vegetables on top.

6. Add sliced avocado, drizzle with tahini dressing, and serve.

Mushroom Risotto

Ingredients:

1½ cups Arborio rice; 250 g mushrooms, sliced; 1 onion, finely chopped; 2 garlic cloves, minced; 5 cups vegetable broth; ½ cup dry white wine or extra broth; 2 tbsp olive oil; 2 tbsp nutritional yeast; salt and pepper; fresh parsley.

How to make it:

1. Heat the vegetable broth in a separate pot and keep it warm.

2. Heat olive oil in a large pan and sauté the onion and mushrooms for 5 minutes.

3. Add garlic and rice and cook for 1–2 minutes.

4. Add the wine and stir until mostly absorbed.

5. Add warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring frequently until the rice becomes creamy and tender.

6. Stir in nutritional yeast, season with salt and pepper, and garnish with parsley.

Sweet Potato Chili

Ingredients:

2 sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed; 1 can kidney beans, drained; 1 can black beans, drained; 1 can diced tomatoes; 1 onion, chopped; 1 bell pepper, chopped; 3 garlic cloves, minced; 2 cups vegetable broth; 1 tbsp chili powder; 1 tsp cumin; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot and sauté the onion and bell pepper for 5 minutes.

2. Add garlic, chili powder, and cumin and cook for 1 minute.

3. Add sweet potatoes, tomatoes, beans, and vegetable broth.

4. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat.

5. Simmer for 25–30 minutes until the sweet potatoes are tender.

6. Season with salt and pepper and serve hot with your favorite vegan toppings.
