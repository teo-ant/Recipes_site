Easy Chocolate Mug Cake

Ingredients:

4 tbsp flour; 2 tbsp sugar; 2 tbsp cocoa powder; ¼ tsp baking powder; 3 tbsp milk; 2 tbsp vegetable oil; ¼ tsp vanilla extract; a pinch of salt; chocolate chips (optional).

How to make it:

1. Add the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt to a microwave-safe mug.

2. Mix in the milk, vegetable oil, and vanilla extract until smooth.

3. Add a few chocolate chips if desired.

4. Microwave for about 60–90 seconds.

5. Let the cake cool for 1–2 minutes before serving.

6. Enjoy warm, optionally topped with powdered sugar or whipped cream.

No-Bake Cheesecake Cups

Ingredients:

150 g digestive biscuits; 250 g cream cheese; 100 ml heavy cream; 3 tbsp sugar; 1 tsp vanilla extract; 100 g fresh berries; 1 tbsp lemon juice.

How to make it:

1. Crush the digestive biscuits into fine crumbs.

2. Whip the heavy cream until soft peaks form.

3. Mix the cream cheese, sugar, and vanilla extract in a bowl.

4. Gently fold the whipped cream into the cream cheese mixture.

5. Add a layer of biscuit crumbs to each cup, followed by the cheesecake mixture.

6. Top with fresh berries and a little lemon juice, then chill for at least 1 hour.

Easy Banana Oat Cookies

Ingredients:

2 ripe bananas; 120 g rolled oats; 1 tsp cinnamon; 1 tsp vanilla extract; 40 g chocolate chips; a pinch of salt.

How to make it:

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and line a baking tray with baking paper.

2. Mash the bananas in a bowl until smooth.

3. Add the oats, cinnamon, vanilla, and salt and mix well.

4. Fold in the chocolate chips.

5. Spoon small portions of the mixture onto the baking tray and flatten them slightly.

6. Bake for 12–15 minutes until lightly golden.

Strawberry Yogurt Parfait

Ingredients:

300 g Greek yogurt; 200 g strawberries, sliced; 3 tbsp honey; 80 g granola; 1 tsp vanilla extract; fresh mint (optional).

How to make it:

1. Mix the Greek yogurt with vanilla extract and 1 tablespoon of honey.

2. Wash and slice the strawberries.

3. Add a layer of yogurt to a glass or dessert cup.

4. Add a layer of strawberries and granola.

5. Repeat the layers until the glass is full.

6. Drizzle with the remaining honey and garnish with fresh mint.

Easy Apple Cinnamon Crumble

Ingredients:

4 apples, peeled and sliced; 2 tbsp sugar; 1 tsp cinnamon; 80 g flour; 60 g rolled oats; 60 g butter; 2 tbsp brown sugar; a pinch of salt.

How to make it:

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).

2. Place the sliced apples in a baking dish and mix them with sugar and cinnamon.

3. In a bowl, combine flour, oats, brown sugar, and salt.

4. Rub the butter into the dry mixture until it becomes crumbly.

5. Sprinkle the crumble mixture evenly over the apples.

6. Bake for 30–35 minutes until golden and bubbling.

3-Ingredient Chocolate Truffles

Ingredients:

200 g dark or milk chocolate; 100 ml heavy cream; 2 tbsp cocoa powder.

How to make it:

1. Chop the chocolate into small pieces and place it in a bowl.

2. Heat the heavy cream until hot but not boiling.

3. Pour the hot cream over the chocolate and let it sit for 2 minutes.

4. Stir until the chocolate is completely melted and smooth.

5. Refrigerate for 1–2 hours until firm.

6. Shape the mixture into small balls and roll them in cocoa powder.

Easy Lemon Mousse

Ingredients:

250 ml heavy cream; 150 g sweetened condensed milk; 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice; zest of 1 lemon; 1 tsp vanilla extract.

How to make it:

1. Whip the heavy cream until soft peaks form.

2. In another bowl, mix the condensed milk, lemon juice, lemon zest, and vanilla.

3. Gently fold the whipped cream into the lemon mixture.

4. Divide the mousse between small glasses or dessert cups.

5. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours.

6. Garnish with lemon zest or a small slice of lemon before serving.

Easy No-Bake Oreo Cheesecake

Ingredients:

200 g Oreo cookies; 60 g melted butter; 250 g cream cheese; 100 ml heavy cream; 3 tbsp powdered sugar; 1 tsp vanilla extract.

How to make it:

1. Crush 150 g of the Oreo cookies into fine crumbs.

2. Mix the crumbs with melted butter and press the mixture into the bottom of a small cake tin or dessert cups.

3. Whip the heavy cream until fluffy.

4. Mix the cream cheese with powdered sugar and vanilla extract.

5. Gently fold the whipped cream into the cream cheese mixture and add some chopped Oreo cookies.

6. Spread the mixture over the biscuit base and refrigerate for at least 3 hours before serving.
