Mediterranean Chicken Bowl

Ingredients:

1 chicken breast; ½ cup quinoa; 1 cucumber, chopped; 2 tomatoes, chopped; 1 can chickpeas, drained; ½ cup feta; ¼ cup olives; ½ cup Greek yogurt; 1 lemon; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 garlic clove, minced; 1 tsp mixed herbs; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Cook the quinoa according to the package instructions.

2. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, garlic, and herbs, then grill or pan-cook until fully cooked.

3. Chop the cucumber and tomatoes.

4. Combine Greek yogurt, lemon juice, and a little olive oil to make the dressing.

5. Put quinoa in a bowl and add sliced chicken, vegetables, chickpeas, olives, and feta.

6. Drizzle with the dressing and serve.

Salmon with Roasted Vegetables

Ingredients:

1 salmon fillet; 1 small head broccoli; 1 bell pepper, chopped; 1 medium sweet potato, chopped; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 lemon; 2 garlic cloves, minced; 1 tsp mixed herbs; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F).

2. Cut the sweet potato and vegetables into small pieces.

3. Toss the vegetables with olive oil, garlic, and herbs.

4. Roast the vegetables for about 20 minutes.

5. Add the salmon to the tray and season with lemon, pepper, and herbs.

6. Bake for another 12–15 minutes, or until the salmon is cooked through.

Chickpea & Avocado Salad

Ingredients:

1 can chickpeas, drained; 1 avocado, chopped; 1 cucumber, chopped; 2 tomatoes, chopped; ½ red onion, chopped; ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped; 1 tbsp lemon juice; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Drain and rinse the chickpeas.

2. Chop the avocado, cucumber, tomatoes, and red onion.

3. Put everything into a large bowl.

4. Add chopped parsley.

5. Mix lemon juice and olive oil and pour over the salad.

6. Toss gently and serve immediately.

Greek Yogurt Berry Oatmeal

Ingredients:

½ cup oats; 1 cup milk or water; ½ cup Greek yogurt; ½ cup blueberries; ½ cup strawberries, sliced; 2 tbsp walnuts, chopped; ½ tsp cinnamon.

How to make it:

1. Combine oats and milk/water in a saucepan.

2. Cook over medium heat for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.

3. Pour into a bowl.

4. Add Greek yogurt and berries.

5. Sprinkle with walnuts and cinnamon.

6. Serve warm.

Lentil & Vegetable Soup

Ingredients:

½ cup lentils; 2 carrots, chopped; 2 celery stalks, chopped; 1 can diced tomatoes; 1 onion, chopped; 1 handful spinach; 2 cups vegetable broth; 2 garlic cloves, minced; 1 tsp mixed herbs; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Sauté chopped onion, carrots, and celery in a pot for 5 minutes.

2. Add garlic and cook for another minute.

3. Add rinsed lentils, tomatoes, vegetable broth, and herbs.

4. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat.

5. Simmer for 25–35 minutes, until the lentils are tender.

6. Stir in spinach during the last few minutes.

7. Season to taste and serve.

Egg & Vegetable Breakfast Wrap

Ingredients:

2 eggs; 1 handful spinach; 1 tomato, chopped; ½ bell pepper, chopped; ½ avocado, sliced; 1 whole-grain tortilla; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Chop the vegetables.

2. Cook the peppers and tomatoes in a pan for a few minutes.

3. Add spinach and cook until wilted.

4. Beat the eggs and pour them into the pan.

5. Stir until the eggs are fully cooked.

6. Place the mixture in a whole-grain tortilla..

7. Add sliced avocado, roll it up, and serve.

Chicken & Sweet Potato Tray Bake

Ingredients:

1 chicken breast; 1 medium sweet potato; 1 zucchini; 1 small head broccoli; 1 onion; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp paprika; 2 garlic cloves, minced; 1 tsp mixed herbs; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.

2. Cut the chicken and vegetables into similar-sized pieces.

3. Place everything on a baking tray.

4. Add olive oil, paprika, garlic, herbs, salt, and pepper.

5. Mix everything together.

6. Bake for 25–30 minutes, until the chicken is fully cooked and the vegetables are tender.

Apple-Cinnamon Chia Pudding

Ingredients:

3 tbsp chia seeds; 1 cup milk; 1 apple, chopped; ½ tsp cinnamon; ¼ cup Greek yogurt (optional); 1 tsp honey (optional).

How to make it:

1. Mix 3 tablespoons of chia seeds with about 200 ml of milk.

2. Add cinnamon and a small amount of honey if desired.

3. Stir well and refrigerate for at least 3–4 hours, preferably overnight.

4. Chop an apple into small pieces.

5. Top the chia pudding with the apple and some Greek yogurt.

6. Sprinkle with extra cinnamon and enjoy.
