Midnight Chocolate Toast

Ingredients:

2 slices of bread; 2 tbsp chocolate spread; 1 banana, sliced; 1 tsp honey; a pinch of cinnamon.

How to make it:

1. Toast the bread until lightly golden.

2. Spread chocolate spread evenly over each slice.

3. Add the banana slices on top.

4. Drizzle with honey.

5. Sprinkle with a little cinnamon.

6. Serve immediately while warm.

Cheesy Garlic Quesadilla

Ingredients:

2 flour tortillas; 100 g grated mozzarella or cheddar; 1 garlic clove, minced; 1 tsp butter; ½ tsp dried oregano; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Mix the grated cheese with garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper.

2. Place one tortilla in a pan over medium heat.

3. Add the cheese mixture over half of the tortilla.

4. Fold the tortilla in half and cook for 2–3 minutes.

5. Flip and cook until golden and the cheese has melted.

6. Cut into triangles and serve hot.

Cinnamon Sugar Mug Cake

Ingredients:

4 tbsp flour; 2 tbsp sugar; ¼ tsp baking powder; 3 tbsp milk; 1 tbsp melted butter; ½ tsp cinnamon; 1 tsp brown sugar; a pinch of salt.

How to make it:

1. Mix flour, sugar, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt in a microwave-safe mug.

2. Add milk and melted butter and stir until smooth.

3. Sprinkle brown sugar and a little extra cinnamon on top.

4. Microwave for 60–90 seconds.

5. Let it cool for about 1 minute.

6. Enjoy warm, optionally with a spoonful of yogurt or ice cream.

Loaded Nachos

Ingredients:

100 g tortilla chips; 100 g grated cheddar; ½ tomato, diced; ¼ red onion, chopped; 2 tbsp sweet corn; 2 tbsp sour cream; fresh jalapeños (optional).

How to make it:

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F).

2. Spread the tortilla chips on a baking tray.

3. Sprinkle cheddar cheese evenly over the chips.

4. Add tomato, red onion, corn, and jalapeños.

5. Bake for 5–7 minutes until the cheese is melted.

6. Add sour cream on top and serve immediately.

Peanut Butter Banana Bites

Ingredients:

2 bananas; 3 tbsp peanut butter; 50 g dark chocolate; 1 tsp coconut oil; a pinch of sea salt.

How to make it:

1. Slice the bananas into small rounds.

2. Spread a little peanut butter on half of the banana slices.

3. Place another banana slice on top to make small sandwiches.

4. Melt the chocolate with coconut oil.

5. Dip or drizzle the banana bites with the melted chocolate.

6. Sprinkle with a little sea salt and refrigerate for 20–30 minutes.

Creamy Instant Ramen

Ingredients:

1 pack instant ramen noodles; 250 ml water; 100 ml milk; 1 egg; 1 tsp soy sauce; 1 tsp sesame oil; 1 spring onion, sliced; chili flakes (optional).

How to make it:

1. Cook the ramen noodles in water according to the package instructions.

2. Add the milk and soy sauce and simmer for 1–2 minutes.

3. Crack the egg into the noodles and stir gently until cooked.

4. Add the seasoning packet and sesame oil.

5. Cook for another 30 seconds.

6. Top with spring onion and chili flakes before serving.

Warm Cinnamon Apples

Ingredients:

2 apples; 1 tbsp butter; 1 tbsp brown sugar; 1 tsp cinnamon; 1 tsp honey; a handful of chopped nuts (optional).

How to make it:

1. Peel and slice the apples.

2. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat.

3. Add the apples, brown sugar, and cinnamon.

4. Cook for 5–7 minutes until the apples become soft.

5. Drizzle with honey.

6. Add chopped nuts if desired and serve warm.

Midnight Grilled Cheese

Ingredients:

2 slices of bread; 2–3 slices of cheddar or mozzarella; 1 tbsp butter; ½ tsp garlic powder; a pinch of black pepper.

How to make it:

1. Spread butter on one side of each bread slice.

2. Place the cheese between the unbuttered sides of the bread.

3. Sprinkle garlic powder and black pepper inside.

4. Heat a pan over medium-low heat.

5. Cook the sandwich for 2–3 minutes on each side until golden and the cheese melts.

6. Cut in half and enjoy while hot.
