Caprese Pasta

Ingredients:

300 g pasta; 250 g cherry tomatoes, halved; 200 g mozzarella, cubed; 2 garlic cloves, minced; 2 tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp dried Italian herbs; salt and pepper; fresh basil.

How to make it:

1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions and reserve ½ cup of pasta water.

2. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.

3. Add garlic and cherry tomatoes and cook for 4–5 minutes.

4. Add the cooked pasta, Italian herbs, and a little pasta water, then toss everything together.

5. Stir in the mozzarella and cook for 1–2 minutes until slightly softened.

6. Season with salt and pepper and garnish with fresh basil.

Mushroom Tacos

Ingredients:

8 small corn or flour tortillas; 300 g mushrooms, sliced; 1 bell pepper, sliced; ½ red onion, sliced; 1 avocado, sliced; 1 tsp cumin; ½ tsp chili powder; 1 lime; fresh cilantro; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Heat a little oil in a pan over medium heat.

2. Add the mushrooms, bell pepper, and red onion and cook for 6–8 minutes.

3. Add cumin, chili powder, salt, and pepper and cook for 1–2 minutes.

4. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan for about 30 seconds on each side.

5. Fill each tortilla with the cooked vegetables and add avocado.

6. Squeeze lime juice over the tacos and garnish with cilantro.

Spinach Lasagna

Ingredients:

9 lasagna sheets; 300 g ricotta cheese; 200 g spinach; 400 g tomato sauce; 150 g mozzarella, grated; 50 g Parmesan, grated; 2 garlic cloves, minced; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper; ½ tsp dried oregano.

How to make it:

1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F) and cook the lasagna sheets according to the package instructions.

2. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the garlic and spinach for 2–3 minutes.

3. Mix the cooked spinach with ricotta, oregano, salt, and pepper.

4. Layer tomato sauce, lasagna sheets, and the spinach-ricotta mixture in a baking dish.

5. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan, then bake for 30–35 minutes.

6. Let the lasagna rest for 5–10 minutes before slicing and serving.

Stuffed Peppers

Ingredients:

4 large bell peppers; 1 cup cooked rice; 1 can black beans, drained; 1 cup corn; 1 tomato, diced; 100 g cheddar cheese, grated; 1 onion, chopped; 1 tsp cumin; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F) and cut the tops off the bell peppers.

2. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the onion for 4–5 minutes.

3. Add rice, black beans, corn, tomato, cumin, salt, and pepper and mix well.

4. Fill each bell pepper with the rice and vegetable mixture.

5. Top the peppers with grated cheddar cheese and bake for 25–30 minutes.

6. Serve hot once the peppers are tender and the cheese is melted.

Spinach Omelette

Ingredients:

4 eggs; 1 cup fresh spinach; ½ bell pepper, diced; ½ onion, chopped; 50 g feta cheese, crumbled; 1 tbsp olive oil; ½ tsp dried oregano; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Whisk the eggs in a bowl with oregano, salt, and pepper.

2. Heat olive oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat.

3. Add onion, bell pepper, and spinach and cook for 3–4 minutes.

4. Pour the eggs over the vegetables and cook until the edges begin to set.

5. Sprinkle feta cheese over one half of the omelette and fold it in half.

6. Cook for another 1–2 minutes, then serve immediately.

Vegetable Curry

Ingredients:

1 zucchini, cubed; 1 bell pepper, chopped; 1 carrot, sliced; 1 cup cauliflower florets; 1 can coconut milk; 1 can diced tomatoes; 1 onion, chopped; 2 garlic cloves, minced; 1 tbsp curry powder; 1 tsp ground cumin; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Heat olive oil in a large pan and sauté the onion for 4–5 minutes.

2. Add garlic, curry powder, and cumin and cook for 1 minute.

3. Add zucchini, bell pepper, carrot, and cauliflower and cook for 5 minutes.

4. Stir in diced tomatoes and coconut milk.

5. Simmer for 15–20 minutes until the vegetables are tender.

6. Season with salt and pepper and serve with rice or warm bread.

Greek Salad

Ingredients:

2 tomatoes, chopped; 1 cucumber, sliced; ½ red onion, sliced; 1 green bell pepper, sliced; 100 g feta cheese, cubed; ½ cup Kalamata olives; 2 tbsp olive oil; 1 tbsp lemon juice; ½ tsp dried oregano; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Wash and chop the tomatoes, cucumber, onion, and bell pepper.

2. Place all the vegetables in a large salad bowl.

3. Add the Kalamata olives and crumbled or cubed feta cheese.

4. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, salt, and pepper in a small bowl.

5. Pour the dressing over the salad and gently toss everything together.

6. Serve immediately or chill for 10 minutes before serving.

Eggplant Parmesan

Ingredients:

1 large eggplant, sliced; 2 eggs; 1 cup breadcrumbs; ½ cup flour; 400 g tomato sauce; 150 g mozzarella, grated; 50 g Parmesan, grated; 1 tsp dried oregano; 2 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F) and season the eggplant slices with salt and pepper.

2. Dip each eggplant slice in flour, then beaten eggs, and finally breadcrumbs.

3. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the slices for 2–3 minutes on each side.

4. Layer tomato sauce, eggplant, mozzarella, and Parmesan in a baking dish.

5. Sprinkle with oregano and bake for 25–30 minutes until golden and bubbling.

6. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving.
