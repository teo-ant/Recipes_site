Creamy Tomato Pasta

Ingredients:

250 g pasta; 200 g cherry tomatoes; 150 ml cooking cream; 2 garlic cloves; 30 g Parmesan; 1 tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp dried oregano; salt and pepper; fresh basil.

How to make it:

1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions and reserve a little pasta water.

2. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the garlic for 1 minute.

3. Add the cherry tomatoes and cook for 5–6 minutes until softened.

4. Pour in the cream and add oregano, salt, and pepper.

5. Add the cooked pasta and Parmesan and mix well.

6. Add a little pasta water if needed and garnish with fresh basil.

Chicken Caesar Wrap

Ingredients:

2 large tortillas; 200 g cooked chicken breast; 2 cups romaine lettuce; 40 g Parmesan; 3 tbsp Caesar dressing; ½ tomato, diced; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Slice the cooked chicken into thin pieces.

2. Chop the lettuce and tomato.

3. Place the lettuce, chicken, tomato, and Parmesan in a bowl.

4. Add Caesar dressing and mix everything together.

5. Divide the mixture between the tortillas.

6. Roll the tortillas tightly and serve immediately.

Mediterranean Couscous Bowl

Ingredients:

150 g couscous; 1 cucumber; 2 tomatoes; ½ red onion; 100 g feta cheese; ½ cup chickpeas; 2 tbsp olive oil; 1 tbsp lemon juice; ½ tsp oregano; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Prepare the couscous according to the package instructions.

2. Chop the cucumber, tomatoes, and red onion.

3. Add the vegetables and chickpeas to a large bowl.

4. Add the cooked couscous and crumbled feta.

5. Mix olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, salt, and pepper to make the dressing.

6. Pour the dressing over the bowl, mix gently, and serve.

Crispy Chicken Rice Bowl

Ingredients:

200 g chicken breast; 150 g cooked rice; 1 carrot; ½ cucumber; ½ avocado; 2 tbsp breadcrumbs; 1 egg; 1 tbsp soy sauce; 1 tsp sesame oil; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces and season with salt and pepper.

2. Dip the chicken in beaten egg and coat with breadcrumbs.

3. Cook the chicken in a lightly oiled pan for 4–5 minutes on each side until crispy.

4. Slice the carrot, cucumber, and avocado.

5. Place the cooked rice in a bowl and arrange the vegetables and chicken on top.

6. Drizzle with soy sauce and sesame oil before serving.

Pesto Chicken Sandwich

Ingredients:

2 ciabatta rolls; 200 g cooked chicken breast; 2 tbsp pesto; 1 tomato; 100 g mozzarella; fresh basil; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Slice the ciabatta rolls in half.

2. Spread pesto over both sides of the bread.

3. Add sliced chicken, tomato, and mozzarella.

4. Season with salt and pepper.

5. Add fresh basil and drizzle with olive oil.

6. Toast the sandwich for 3–4 minutes if desired, then serve.

Creamy Garlic Gnocchi

Ingredients:

400 g gnocchi; 2 garlic cloves; 150 ml cooking cream; 50 g Parmesan; 1 tbsp butter; 1 tsp dried Italian herbs; salt and pepper; fresh parsley.

How to make it:

1. Cook the gnocchi according to the package instructions.

2. Melt the butter in a pan and sauté the garlic for 1 minute.

3. Add the cooking cream and Italian herbs.

4. Stir in the Parmesan and season with salt and pepper.

5. Add the cooked gnocchi and mix until coated in the sauce.

6. Garnish with fresh parsley and extra Parmesan.

Tuna Avocado Salad

Ingredients:

1 can tuna, drained; 1 avocado; 1 cucumber; 2 tomatoes; ½ red onion; 2 tbsp olive oil; 1 tbsp lemon juice; salt and pepper; fresh parsley.

How to make it:

1. Drain the tuna and place it in a large bowl.

2. Chop the avocado, cucumber, tomatoes, and red onion.

3. Add all the vegetables to the tuna.

4. Mix olive oil and lemon juice in a small bowl.

5. Pour the dressing over the salad and season with salt and pepper.

6. Mix gently and garnish with fresh parsley.

Cheesy Baked Potatoes

Ingredients:

4 medium potatoes; 100 g cheddar cheese; 50 g mozzarella; 2 tbsp sour cream; 1 spring onion; 1 tbsp olive oil; ½ tsp paprika; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F).

2. Wash the potatoes, pierce them with a fork, and rub them with olive oil, salt, and pepper.

3. Bake for 40–50 minutes until tender.

4. Cut each potato open and fluff the inside with a fork.

5. Add cheddar, mozzarella, sour cream, and paprika.

6. Bake for another 5 minutes until the cheese melts, then top with spring onion.
