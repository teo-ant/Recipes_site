Eggs Benedict

Ingredients:

2 English muffins; 4 eggs; 4 slices of ham; 2 egg yolks; 100 g butter, melted; 1 tbsp lemon juice; salt and pepper; fresh chives.

How to make it:

1. Toast the English muffin halves until golden.

2. Warm the ham in a pan for 1–2 minutes.

3. Poach the eggs in simmering water for about 3–4 minutes.

4. Whisk the egg yolks with lemon juice over low heat.

5. Slowly add the melted butter while whisking until the sauce becomes creamy.

6. Place ham and poached eggs on the toasted muffins and top with hollandaise sauce and chives.

Avocado Brunch Toast

Ingredients:

2 slices sourdough bread; 1 ripe avocado; 2 eggs; 50 g feta cheese; 1 tsp lemon juice; 1 tbsp olive oil; chili flakes; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Toast the sourdough bread until crispy.

2. Mash the avocado with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.

3. Cook the eggs fried or poached, depending on preference.

4. Spread the avocado over the toast.

5. Add the eggs and crumble feta cheese on top.

6. Finish with olive oil and chili flakes.

Berry Ricotta Pancakes

Ingredients:

150 g ricotta cheese; 2 eggs; 100 g flour; 100 ml milk; 1 tbsp sugar; 1 tsp baking powder; 1 tsp vanilla extract; 100 g mixed berries; maple syrup.

How to make it:

1. Whisk the ricotta, eggs, milk, sugar, and vanilla together.

2. Add the flour and baking powder and mix gently.

3. Heat a lightly buttered pan over medium heat.

4. Pour small portions of batter into the pan.

5. Cook for 2–3 minutes on each side until golden.

6. Stack the pancakes and serve with berries and maple syrup.

Brunch Breakfast Burrito

Ingredients:

2 large flour tortillas; 4 eggs; 100 g black beans; 80 g grated cheddar; ½ avocado; ½ bell pepper; ¼ red onion; 2 tbsp salsa; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the bell pepper and onion for 3–4 minutes.

2. Add the black beans and cook for another minute.

3. Scramble the eggs in the same pan and season with salt and pepper.

4. Warm the tortillas for a few seconds.

5. Fill each tortilla with eggs, vegetables, cheese, avocado, and salsa.

6. Fold the sides inward and roll tightly into a burrito.

French Toast with Caramelized Bananas

Ingredients:

4 thick slices of brioche; 2 eggs; 100 ml milk; 1 tsp vanilla extract; ½ tsp cinnamon; 1 tbsp butter; 2 bananas; 1 tbsp brown sugar; 1 tbsp maple syrup.

How to make it:

1. Whisk the eggs, milk, vanilla, and cinnamon in a bowl.

2. Dip each brioche slice into the mixture.

3. Melt the butter in a pan and cook the bread for 2–3 minutes on each side.

4. Remove the French toast and add sliced bananas to the pan.

5. Sprinkle the bananas with brown sugar and cook for 2–3 minutes until caramelized.

6. Serve the French toast topped with caramelized bananas and maple syrup.

Shakshuka

Ingredients:

4 eggs; 400 g canned diced tomatoes; 1 bell pepper; 1 onion; 2 garlic cloves; 1 tsp paprika; ½ tsp cumin; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper; fresh parsley.

How to make it:

1. Heat olive oil in a large pan.

2. Sauté the onion and bell pepper for 5–6 minutes.

3. Add garlic, paprika, and cumin and cook for 1 minute.

4. Stir in the tomatoes and simmer for 8–10 minutes.

5. Make four small wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each one.

6. Cover and cook for 5–7 minutes until the egg whites are set. Garnish with parsley.

Caprese Brunch Sandwich

Ingredients:

2 ciabatta rolls; 1 tomato, sliced; 125 g fresh mozzarella; fresh basil leaves; 1 tbsp pesto; 1 tbsp olive oil; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Slice the ciabatta rolls in half.

2. Spread pesto over the inside of each roll.

3. Add slices of tomato and fresh mozzarella.

4. Add fresh basil leaves.

5. Season with salt and pepper and drizzle with olive oil.

6. Close the sandwich and serve immediately or lightly toast it before serving.

Creamy Mushroom Omelette

Ingredients:

3 eggs; 150 g mushrooms, sliced; 50 g cream cheese; 1 tbsp butter; 1 garlic clove, minced; 1 tbsp fresh parsley; salt and pepper.

How to make it:

1. Melt the butter in a non-stick pan.

2. Add the mushrooms and garlic and cook for 4–5 minutes.

3. Whisk the eggs with salt and pepper.

4. Pour the eggs over the mushrooms and cook until the edges begin to set.

5. Add the cream cheese to one half of the omelette.

6. Fold the omelette, cook for another minute, and garnish with fresh parsley.
